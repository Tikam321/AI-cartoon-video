# 🎬 AI Mythological YouTube Channel Automation Guide
A comprehensive, step-by-step framework to create high-yielding, 15-to-20-minute continuous AI-animated Hindu mythological videos optimized for the Indian audience.

---

## 🛠️ The Master AI Tool Stack

| Production Step | Recommended AI Model / Tool | Primary Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Scripting** | DeepSeek-V3 / Claude 3.5 Sonnet | Writing Hindi dialogues and formatting structured English image prompts. |
| **2. Characters & Art** | Google Flow (Nano Banana 2) / Midjourney | Generating hyper-realistic or Pixar-style characters and temple assets. |
| **3. Video Generation** | Kling AI / Luma Dream Machine | Converting static images into high-quality 5-10 second moving clips. |
| **4. Voiceovers** | ElevenLabs | Creating deep, theatrical Hindi voice acting profiles. |
| **5. Lip-Syncing** | Hedra AI / Sync Labs | Automatically matching character lip movements to the Hindi audio track. |
| **6. Final Assembly** | CapCut Desktop | Stitching all segments, auto-generating subtitles, and exporting the final video. |

## 📅 The 4-Day Production Workflow

To build a 20-minute video without getting overwhelmed, group your tasks by function instead of building clip-by-clip. A 20-minute video will require roughly **80 to 100 short scenes**.

### 🌟 Day 1: Scripting & Concept Blueprint (Time: 1 Hour)
1. Open your text AI (DeepSeek or Claude).
2. Paste a highly specific prompt asking for a detailed scene-by-scene script breakout.
3. Ensure the AI gives you a clean table layout containing four distinct columns:
   * **Scene & Timecode:** Tracking the sequence of the video.
   * **Voiceover Text:** The exact Hindi script for the narrator/characters.
   * **Visual Scene Description:** A simple guide for you to understand the context.
   * **Image Generation Prompt:** The complex, detailed art prompt written specifically for the AI art generator.

### 🎨 Day 2: Character Seeds & Image Batching (Time: 2 Hours)
1. **Establish the Character Seed:** Open your image generator (e.g., Google Flow). Generate your main character (e.g., Lakshmi Mata) on a solid, clean, plain gray background showing multiple angles (front, side, 3/4 profile). Download this image. This is your master seed.
2. **Batch Produce Scene Backgrounds:** Move down your script table from Step 1, line by line. 
3. **Execute Step 3 (The Combination):** Upload your master character seed into the generator's "Character Reference / Image Prompt" slot. Right next to it, copy and paste the text from the **Image Generation Prompt** column for Scene 1. Hit generate.
4. **Save Systematically:** Download the result and save it as `Scene_1.png`. Immediately repeat the process for Scene 2, saving the output as `Scene_2.png`. Repeat this loop until you have all 80-100 images saved in a single folder.

### 🔊 Day 3: Video Clips, Voiceovers & Lip-Syncing (Time: 2.5 Hours)
1. **Generate Motion:** Upload `Scene_1.png` into your video model (Kling or Luma). Type a clean camera instruction in English (e.g., *"Cinematic slow-motion zoom-in, glowing particles drifting softly"*). Download the clip as `Clip_1.mp4`. Repeat for all images.
2. **Generate Audio:** Copy the text from the Hindi Voiceover column in your script. Paste it into ElevenLabs. Select a deep, emotional, resonant voice model. Generate and download the MP3 tracks.
3. **Automate Lip-Syncing:** Identify the specific clips where a character is actively speaking on camera. Open your Lip-Sync AI tool (Hedra or Sync Labs). Upload the specific scene video clip and drop the corresponding ElevenLabs character audio track right next to it. Hit sync and download the freshly animated speaking clip.

### ✂️ Day 4: Final Editing & Assembly (Time: 1.5 Hours)
1. **Build the Audio Blueprint:** Open CapCut Desktop. Import all your generated voiceover tracks and drop them onto the audio timeline first. This gives you your complete 20-minute layout timeline.
2. **Sequence the Video Clips:** Drag `Clip_1.mp4` to the beginning of the video timeline. Stretch or trim the clip so it aligns perfectly with the length of the voiceover for Scene 1. Drag `Clip_2.mp4` right next to it. Continue placing all clips in perfect sequential order (1 to 100) until the entire 20-minute audio track is visually covered.
3. **Run Auto-Subtitles:** Go to the `Text` panel -> click `Auto Captions` -> select the language spoken in your audio track. The software will instantly track the voice and overlay perfectly timed subtitles across the entire timeline. Format the text with high-visibility colors (e.g., Yellow font with a bold Black stroke outline).
4. **Add Ambient Audio:** Download a copyright-free Indian traditional flute or cinematic ambient background score from the YouTube Audio Library. Drop it into a secondary audio layer and reduce its volume to **-20dB** so it does not overpower the dialogue.
5. **Master Export:** Click `Export` in the top right corner. Select **1080p or 4K resolution**, set the frame rate to **30fps**, and export your single, continuous 20-minute masterpiece ready for YouTube.

## 💰 The YouTube Optimization Strategy
* **Mid-Roll Monetization:** Because your video crosses the 8-minute mark, manually place an advertisement every 3 to 4 minutes inside your YouTube Studio upload settings to multiply your RPM earnings.
* **High-CTR Thumbnails:** Use Canva to design thumbnails. Crop a sharp, close-up, high-definition image of the main deity's face, apply a bright golden inner/outer glow filter, and add large, bold Hindi text using warning tones like Red or Yellow (e.g., **"दीवाली पर यह भूल मत करना!"**).
